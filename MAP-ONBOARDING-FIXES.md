# Camera stability, floor transition, onboarding and branding refinement

- The indoor camera is bounded by a zoom-dependent envelope; panning and pinch gestures cannot send the building out of the viewport.
- Cinematic overview uses a safe zoom of 0.96 instead of 0.88; it re-centers and keeps its selected position visible.
- Manual floor changes interrupt cinematic timers. Floor switches cross-fade and move architectural layers gently, with motion reduction support.
- Pinch-to-drag handoff rebases to the remaining pointer to prevent a sudden jump.
- Added a subtle level indicator and replaced brightly stacked floor flashes with a 440ms architectural transition.
- Onboarding now has neutral surfaces, restrained green accents, smaller playful animations, and updated typography.
- Replaced the low-quality in-hero university logo treatment with a transparent version of the supplied university wordmark on a clean white surface. ChatUAB is independently made and is not university-affiliated.
- No changes to the actual location data or the indicative nature of the indoor floor geometry.
